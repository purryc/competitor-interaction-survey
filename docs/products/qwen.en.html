Software Landscape and the Boundary Between Old and New

The current App version is 7.4.2. The August update explicitly identifies Work Assistant, thinking/research, voice, and brand agents. The store covers finished Office deliverables, web applications, one-off/recurring scheduled tasks, learning with board-style explanations, homework marking, real-time notes, everyday services, and images/video. Work Assistant monthly in-app purchases are listed at RMB 19 and RMB 49, with a separate creative membership. A free App does not mean every entitlement is free.A1 Alibaba's announcement on 2026-01-15 confirmed integrations with Taobao, instant retail, Alipay, Fliggy, and Amap, and a public beta in China. “Task Assistant” was an invitation-only beta at the time. The 40 cups of milk tea and phone-based restaurant booking in the announcement were official live demonstrations, not evidence of ordinary consumers' success rates.A2 The formal user agreement lists Windows/Mac/HarmonyOS and other forms; the desktop product includes browsing/a sidebar. Specific versions and account entitlements still need verification on each platform.A3

Four Complete Scenarios

  1. Food-order fulfillment (official + individual account): State the food/address → choose a merchant and item options → confirm the order and payment → delivery. In a June 19 review, Eliyas伊伊 describes around 20 food-delivery experiences, with discounts clearly part of the motivation. The review contains substantial promotional language and cannot establish reliable automatic understanding.A1, A2
  2. From travel planning to a transaction (official): Provide dates/budget for multiple cities → receive an itinerary and transport/accommodation comparisons → proceed to ticket/hotel booking and payment. Inventory, prices, and cancellation policies must be verified; a travel plan is not a completed booking.A2
  3. Work deliverables (currently official): Attachments/goal → Work Assistant → research, calculation, or generation → downloadable Office/web output → user review. Scheduled tasks extend one-off generation into recurring collection and delivery.A1
  4. Restaurant inquiry (official January demonstration): Task Assistant calls a restaurant to ask about child seats → returns a call transcript. This only establishes that the chain existed in the demonstration version; there is no current hands-on verification that all accounts can use it.A2

Evidence of Actual Experience

SSPAI's “10,000-Character Analysis: An In-depth Experience Report on the Qianwen App (2026)” combines experience and commentary, mentioning unreliable automatic web-search triggering, input lag, promotional-event services, and other issues. It is not entirely composed of firsthand samples, and its collected assertions must not be treated as experiments conducted for this report. SMZDM's January 25 “Testing Alibaba Qianwen for Food Delivery” is a repost from a WeChat Official Account. Its summary says understanding failed despite explicit instructions. This is a negative example from an early version and must not be extrapolated into the current success rate.A4, A5

The glasses' companion “Qianwen AI Wearables” (千问AI穿戴) App supports pairing, permissions/battery status, firmware, photo import, conversation history, navigation, and translation. Reviewer Paulo172 (April 27) says an English-language system cannot be switched and the assistant replies before they finish longer sentences. This is a specific negative example involving language and turn-taking control.A6 Posts about extended wear by early S1 users say the center of gravity and replaceable battery help, while the display sits high, holds little text, relies on a phone and network, and limits WeChat replies. Reposted material can only serve as a lead to the original creator's report. “Unlimited battery life” must not be interpreted physically as requiring no power supply.A7, A8
