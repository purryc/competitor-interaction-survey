Capabilities and Boundaries

The current official App Store description covers search, image editing/video, voice calls, recording transcription and translation, close reading of files, code execution, and Tencent's content ecosystem. September updates added photos within recordings, travel and diet assistants in Expert Mode, and explanations with dynamic board writing. Searching WeChat Official Accounts and Channels is a source advantage; it does not mean the assistant can operate a WeChat account without restriction. Entry point: https://yuanbao.tencent.com/. The China store is the version reference for this research; not every mobile feature can automatically be assumed to exist on Web/PC.Y1

Specific Uses

  1. Classroom/meeting records (current official workflow): Open the AI voice recorder → photograph projections/whiteboards while recording → save linked audio, images, and text → generate meeting notes → share to Tencent Docs or WorkBuddy. The research focus is the usable record produced after a meeting, not merely voice input.Y1
  2. Serialized fiction (individual account): Reviewer “1119383839494” (page date: May 3) says they use Yuanbao to write novels and publish on Fanqie (番茄); their company uses Yuanbao Pai (元宝派), but they use Qianwen for market research and request finished office deliverables/research capabilities. This reveals a workflow spanning multiple products. A user's request must not be presented as a feature Yuanbao already had at that time.Y1
  3. Work log (individual account): Reviewer “-11123444” (April 1) uses conversations as a work log and repository of important matters. They like the reading layout but want to retain only answers and delete questions to make information easier to find. The pain point is knowledge organization and retrieval, rather than initial generation.Y1
  4. Travel decisions (official September update): Enter a destination and requirements in Expert Mode → receive an illustrated itinerary → review attractions/transport → follow links to book hotels/tickets. “Opening a booking destination” must be distinguished from automatically completing payment.Y1

Experience Assessment

The strengths are Tencent information sources and destinations for content delivery. A user repurposing a single conversation history as a work log indicates a continuing need for organization/archiving. Generic claims in reviews such as “all-purpose” and “unlimited” are not used as factual evidence; a free store listing does not mean unlimited use forever. The privacy page is self-reported by the developer and has not been verified by Apple; it is not an independent security certification.Y1
