vivo BlueLM Xiao V

Phone memory and writing, the PC office suite, watch assistants and newly announced Pro/Cowork, with concrete versions and task boundaries.

Sources checked: 6 October 2026, primarily mainland-China Chinese-language products. Public-source research, not installation or standardized device testing; announcements, previews, staged OTA and documented availability are distinguished.

Software Map

  • OriginOS5/6 baseline: Circle-to-search/semantic search, Xiao V Memory, and conversational creation/drawing. Xiao V Writing is integrated into Atomic Notes, vivo Documents, Mail, and the input method, offering continuation/polishing/rewriting/summaries. The X200 series has official instructions for opening circle-to-search; individual functions are not uniformly available on all older phones. V1, V2
  • Form filling: Xiao V Visa on the X Fold5 generates forms for 42 countries and uses personal information previously stored in Xiao V Memory. It automatically fills only some fields; it does not conduct visa adjudication, guarantee every field is correct, or submit applications automatically. App encryption must be enabled manually by the user; official materials explain sensitive-information encryption and biometric verification. V3
  • PC office suite: Provides BlueLM Xiao V Q&A/drawing/copywriting, Xiao V Writing in Atomic Notes, and phone–PC materials collaboration. It does not depend on purchasing a particular vivo laptop. Cloud synchronization and device interconnection require the correct account/connection. V4, VR1
  • Newly announced on 2026-09-16: OriginOS7's Xiao V Pro proposes task decomposition and cross-app/device operations. Xiao V Cowork lets users view and handle PC-agent tasks from a phone; the AI calendar gathers to-dos from notes and meetings; and the input method can recommend materials based on chat context. This is official announcement evidence, insufficient to show that every existing model receives all features after upgrading. BlueCode is explicitly a preview, and the on-device 30B MoE model is explicitly exploratory research; neither is listed as a generally available consumer capability. V5

Four Task Workflows

  1. Find the right wording for a reply: Open the vivo input method in a chat → ask Xiao V Writing to read the context → choose generation/polishing → tap to insert once satisfied → send the message yourself. AI places text in the edit box; it does not automatically send the message. V1
  2. Continue a document across devices: Draft in Atomic Notes on the phone and enable cloud synchronization → access it in the PC office suite with the same vivo account → ask Xiao V to continue/polish → revise → view it again on the phone. If an original Word document lacks the same Xiao V Writing entry point, the suite's assistant can be invoked separately; this does not mean every Office program embeds it natively. V4, VR1
  3. Prepare visa materials: Wake Xiao V on an X Fold5 → specify the country and generate the form → select quick fill → scan/select information already stored in memory → check populated fields → complete the remaining fields and download locally. The official promise covers only fields corresponding to available information, and encryption must be actively enabled. Submission, legal declarations, and appointments are outside the verified workflow. V3
  4. Ask travel or exercise questions from the wrist: Connect/update a compatible phone and WATCH → open BlueLM Xiao V on the watch → ask about the weather/general knowledge or create a running plan → view the response/task on the watch → begin exercising yourself. “Supports pairing with iOS” must not be interpreted to mean iOS supports the full Xiao V or phone-assistant feature set. V7, V8

Firsthand Experiences and Gaps

  • 看着月亮望着你, vivo Community Cool User management team (酷客管理组), 2025-02-15: An illustrated demonstration of Atomic Notes cloud synchronization, phone–PC clipboard sharing, and Xiao V Writing on both devices. The author is affiliated with the vendor community, and no disclosure establishing commercial independence was found; this is C-level evidence. It can corroborate operating methods, not provide unbiased satisfaction evidence from an ordinary user. VR1
  • 锦衣后卫, vivo's 100-person WATCH5 testing program, page timestamp May 28 at 20:07, with 2025 inferred from the product period: Explicitly received a review sample, observed light weight and battery life, and broadly described Xiao V as smarter, without reporting specific AI task outcomes. It must be labeled “sample relationship/not an independent AI reliability test.” Praise for hardware battery life cannot establish that the assistant works well. VR2
  • Gaps: This research found no reliable independent long-term task logs for Xiao V Pro/Cowork. Official announcements and community tutorials cannot substitute for execution success rates. Nor was there sufficient evidence that the latest vivo earbuds independently support the full BlueLM Xiao V feature set, so earbud-agent capabilities are not invented.
